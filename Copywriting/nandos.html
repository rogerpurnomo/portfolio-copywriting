01 // The Connection

Building Trust & Relationships

Building trust, relationships, and a strong network is key. What started as a simple cold email from a student turned into a long-lasting connection.

I learned firsthand that checking in and providing excellent after-sales service is what truly matters to keep a corporate partnership going strong.

02 // The Strategy

Writing a Winning Proposal

Securing Nando's required a strong, focused, and engaging pitch. Here are the core elements I ensured were in the proposal:

  • ✓ Make it about them

    The absolute biggest mistake in outreach is spending 80% of the email talking about your company. Reverse it. Focus on what their brand or audience stands to gain.

  • ✓ Be specific, not vague

    Don't just say "let's collaborate." Offer one or two concrete ideas so they have something tangible to react to. It shows you actually did your homework.

  • ✓ Keep the CTA low-pressure

    Asking for a "10-minute chat to see if it's a fit" feels much safer and requires less commitment than asking for a formal meeting or proposal review.
