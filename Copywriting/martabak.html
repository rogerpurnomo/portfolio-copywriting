Martabak Bangka 66 Growth Project

Type

Data-backed social media marketing and physical retail expansion.

Objective

Scale the brand's online presence to drive real-world foot traffic and open new branch locations.

Scroll

01 // The Digital Scale

Hitting 53.8K+ on TikTok.

We didn't just post randomly and hope for the best. I dug into our TikTok analytics to see exactly what the audience was engaging with.

By analyzing watch times and interaction rates, I wrote targeted ad plans and content scripts that pushed the account to over 53.8K loyal followers. It's solid proof that using data makes content go viral.

02 // Physical Expansion

Data-Oriented Scaling from 3 to 5 Branches.

The Revenue Goal

The main mission was to scale up the company profile and generate enough revenue to expand our footprint from 3 branches to 5. But expansion is risky without validation.

Survey-Backed Decisions

Instead of guessing where to go, I conducted customer surveys to make a data-oriented decision on new locations and test new menu items.

This direct feedback led us to open the new Padang Jawa branch and strategically relocate to Bandar Utama.

03 // The Launch

Ads, Events & Collaborations.

Opening a branch is only half the battle; getting people there is the rest. I planned out specific advertising events and designed promotional graphics to build hype. By setting up local collaborations, we gained massive exposure and ensured the new branches had a strong, profitable launch day.
